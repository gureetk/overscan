A lineup is what an account's devices and players see of live TV. A copy starts with this lineup's channels, groups, numbers and edits, and then has its own. A new lineup of your own groups starts empty, and holds only the categories that its groups take, from any source; merged copies of a channel play as one, the others as its backups, and a rule on its Rules tab can be changed or removed. Each account chooses its lineup on the Accounts page.

{{template "i-download"}}Export every lineup

The file keeps what was changed in each lineup, and its rules, to keep or to take to another server. Importing one adds its lineups as new ones, and changes none that are here.
