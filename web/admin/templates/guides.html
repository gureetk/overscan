Guides

Guides of your own, for the channels whose sources give them none. Overscan reads each XMLTV guide on its schedule, and gives a channel without programmes from its own source those of the guide's channel with its guide ID, or else with its name. Where two of the guides' channels have the name, as the same channel in two countries can, a country tag in the channel's name or group chooses; where none does, the channel gets no guide rather than the wrong one. A guide's address can hold a login, so Overscan keeps it encrypted and shows only where it points.
