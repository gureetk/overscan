# Code of conduct

Overscan is a small project with one maintainer. Whoever takes part in it, in issues, pull requests and discussions, or wherever they speak for the project, is expected to:

- be kind and patient with others, whatever their experience, background or identity;
- say what they think of the work, not of the person, and give reasons when they disagree;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise;
- keep others' private information private: their addresses, logins, playlists and logs, and anything sent privately;
- keep to what the project is for: it helps with no reselling of access, no sharing of accounts against a provider's terms, and no reaching of content that someone is not entitled to.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, threats, personal attacks, sexual language or images, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove what breaks this, close issues and pull requests, and block from the project those who keep breaking it, after a warning where one fits.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately, without posting the details in public. GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, under the repository's **Security** tab, reaches only the maintainer, and can be used for this too: say that it is about conduct. What you report stays between you and the maintainer.
